# Recruiting Timeline
> Use when the student asks how MBA summer-internship recruiting unfolds or when they should do what; stay general, never invent dates.

Explain the phases, not dates. Every firm, industry and school sets its own calendar, and it changes between years.

1. **Explore and target:** choose functions and industries; build the target list with priorities.
2. **Network:** company presentations, coffee chats, alumni calls. This usually starts well before applications open.
3. **Prepare materials:** resume, cover letters where asked, core stories, case or technical practice.
4. **Apply:** some programs open on fixed dates with hard deadlines; others review applications on a rolling basis. Structured programs (for example consulting or banking) tend to recruit earlier; many smaller firms and startups hire closer to the summer. Treat these as tendencies to verify, not rules.
5. **Interview:** one or more rounds, sometimes a final "superday".
6. **Offers and decisions:** offers can carry response deadlines; the school's career office may have its own rules about them.

How to verify each firm's dates:
- the firm's official careers page (paste the link on Today: the app records the date exactly as written);
- the school's career office calendar and recruiting policies;
- people at the firm (coffee chats);
- after a cycle ends, re-check: last year's dates are not this year's.

Never state a month or date for a firm unless it is in the student's records with its source; tracked deadlines are in the Action plan.
