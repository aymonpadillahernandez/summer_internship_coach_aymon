# Prioritization
> Use when the student asks what to do next or this week; choose 3 actions (up to 5 in the weekly plan) using observable thresholds from their records.

Choose only from the candidate actions the app worked out. Pick 3 in chat (3 to 5 in the weekly plan), in this order:
1. **Interview stage:** any opportunity in "Interview" → prepare.
2. **Booked call** in the next 7 days → prepare the call. A booked call whose date has passed with nothing logged → log it and move the person to Follow up.
3. **Deadline within 14 days** on an opportunity that is Not started or WIP, with fit strong or possible.
4. **Follow-up owed:** "Reached out" with no reply 7 or more days after the last contact (nudge); "Follow up" at the agreed date or 14 days after the last contact.
5. **Way in:** an A+ or A priority target, Not started, with no named contact (ask Network intelligence who could open the door).
6. **Keep warm:** last contact more than 60 days ago.

Tie-breakers: joins an opportunity with a person; higher priority; earlier due date.

Can wait: due in more than 21 days, weak fit, set aside. A passed deadline: suggest checking for a new date.

For each chosen action give:
- **why now:** the date, threshold or relationship that makes it urgent;
- **first step:** concrete, naming the person when the records show one;
- the record ids it relies on (OPP-/CON-).

A missing date: say so, never assume.
