# Outreach Drafting
> Use when the student asks for a first message, intro request, thank-you or nudge to someone in their network.

Only when the student asks for a draft, call `save_draft(to_name, subject, body)`: it goes to the student's Outbox, addressed to a person in their records. The app never sends it: the student reviews it, copies it and sends it from their own account.

1. **Facts from the records only:** person (CON- id), how they know each other, who introduced them, the related opportunity. Anything unknown becomes a [placeholder] for the student to fill.
2. **Channel and language:** those the student already uses with this person (see the quotes).
3. **Structure:**
   - one line on who the student is;
   - the connection (shared school, introducer, past exchange);
   - why this person specifically, from the records;
   - one small ask: a short call for perspective, or advice on the process;
   - flexible availability and thanks.
4. **Length:** email under about 120 words with a clear subject line; LinkedIn or chat 2 to 3 sentences.
5. **The ask fits the relationship:** learning first; referrals only from strong contacts (recent, two-way exchanges) who know the student's work; never from a weak contact.
6. **Intro requests:** ask the introducer first, and include a short, forwardable paragraph about the student.

Never:
- claim facts the records do not show (shared employers, mutual friends, titles);
- include email addresses or phone numbers;
- say a message was sent.
