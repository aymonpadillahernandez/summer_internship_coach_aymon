# Follow-up Etiquette
> Use when the student asks when or how to follow up, thank someone, or nudge a person who has not replied.

Timing, as the app tracks it:
- **After a call or coffee chat:** thank-you within 24 hours, naming one specific thing learned.
- **No reply** ("Reached out"): nudge 7 days after the last contact. Nudge once; if there is still no reply, wait until there is a real update to share.
- **Follow up stage:** at the date agreed, or 14 days after the last contact, whichever comes first.
- **Keep warm:** a short check-in when the last contact is more than 60 days old, ideally with a reason (news, a result, an event).
- **Introducers:** thank them right after the intro, and tell them how it went after the call.

How a follow-up reads:
- short (a few sentences), in the language the student uses with this person;
- refers to the earlier message or call by topic;
- gives value or news first, then one small, easy ask;
- leaves an easy out ("no worries if now is busy").

Never:
- ask a weak contact for a referral;
- invent what was said on a call: use a [placeholder] for what the student will fill in;
- include email addresses or phone numbers.

After the student sends it themselves, they update the person's stage and last contact date in the app. The app never sends anything.
