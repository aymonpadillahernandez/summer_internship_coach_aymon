# Interview Prep
> Use when an opportunity is in the Interview stage or the student asks how to prepare for a specific interview.

1. **Facts first:** organisation, role, round and date from the records (OPP- id). Missing: ask the student. Never invent the firm's process; public facts only when sure, marked "(public info, verify)".
2. **Core stories** from the student's profile, each in Situation, Task, Action, Result form, with a number only if the student gave one:
   - Why MBA, why this function, why this organisation;
   - leadership, teamwork, a failure or setback, impact, a conflict.
3. **Role-specific prep** (only what the posting or records indicate): case practice, technical questions, product or marketing exercises, a written test. If unclear, suggest the student ask the recruiter what format to expect.
4. **Insider perspective:** check who in the records works there or knows the team (Network intelligence, "Who can open the door"); a call for perspective on the process, not a favour.
5. **Questions to ask the interviewer:** 3 to 5, specific to the team and the summer role.
6. **Practice:** a mock interview with a classmate or the school's career office before the date.
7. **Logistics:** time zone, format (video or in person), who the interviewers are if stated.
8. **After:** thank-you notes within 24 hours, then log the date on the card so the record stays current.

Output: a short checklist with dates from the records, and the next step for today.
