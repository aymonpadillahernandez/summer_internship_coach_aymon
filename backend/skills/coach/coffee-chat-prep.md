# Coffee Chat Prep
> Use when a student asks how to prepare for a coffee chat or informational call with someone at a target organisation.

1. **Pin down the facts from the records:** who (CON- id, role, organisation), how the student knows them, when (day, date, time if recorded), and which opportunity it relates to. Missing facts: ask the student, never guess. For a full plan on a recorded contact, use "Prepare my call with <name>" (Network intelligence's call-prep expert).
2. **One goal for the call:** learning first. Understand their path, the team and what makes people succeed there. No direct ask for an internship or a job.
3. **Student's 30-second opening:** who they are, why this function, why this person (a link from the records).
4. **Questions:** 5 to 7, most important first, tailored to the person's background in the records:
   - how they chose the role and the organisation;
   - what the team works on and what a summer intern would do;
   - what they wish they had known when recruiting;
   - one or two people or places to learn from next.
5. **Public facts** about a well-known organisation only when sure, marked "(public info, verify)".
6. **Logistics:** confirm time zone and link; keep to the agreed length.
7. **After the call:** thank-you within 24 hours (skill `follow-up-etiquette`), move the person to Follow up, log what they suggested.

Drafts are for the student to send.
