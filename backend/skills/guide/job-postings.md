# Job Postings and Careers Pages
> Use when a student wants to add a posting or careers page: public links are read by the app, closed job boards need pasted text.

**Public careers page or posting:** paste the link on Today. The Opportunity scout reads the page itself, without hidden text, and files the opportunity with the page as its source.

**Handshake, LinkedIn Jobs, 12twenty, Symplicity or school career sites:** copy the posting's text and paste it. Their terms forbid automated reading (see docs/research/channels.md).

When a link fails, the app says why; the fix is always to paste the text:
- "This site asks automated readers not to read that page (robots.txt)."
- "This site blocks automated reading (HTTP …)." The app never bypasses bot protection.
- "That link is not a web page" (for example a PDF link): download it and upload the .pdf on Setup, or paste the text.
- Private, local or non-standard addresses are never read.

What to include in a paste: the title, organisation, location, eligibility lines, dates (opening, deadline) and the posting's link if it is shown.

After it is read, check the card on Opportunities:
- deadline copied exactly as written;
- MBA eligibility (Yes / No / Not clear) and fit;
- the items the app rejected and why (for example a quote not found in the text).

Pages behind a login cannot be read (the app never signs in anywhere): paste their text.
