# Calendar Invites
> Use when a student has a booked call or meeting in a calendar invite and wants it recorded with its date and the person.

Why an upload: reading Google Calendar needs Google's verification, and school Microsoft accounts do not allow it, so the app reads the invite file instead.

Steps:
1. Open the invite email → download `invite.ics` (or export one event from the calendar app).
2. Setup tab → "Calendar invites (booked calls)" card → upload the .ics.
3. The Inbox organizer records the call with its date and the person.

Check after the upload (Networking tab):
- The person is in "Call booked" with the call's date and time.
- If no date could be read, the app saves them as "Follow up" instead (a booked call must carry its date): ask the student to add the date on the contact.
- The person is linked to the opportunity the call is about.

Next:
- Before the call: in Ask your coach, type "Prepare my call with <name>" (Network intelligence's call-prep expert).
- After the call: move the person to "Follow up" and log what was agreed; the Action plan shows "Log how it went" once the booked date has passed.

One event per file works best; a whole-calendar export carries unrelated events.
