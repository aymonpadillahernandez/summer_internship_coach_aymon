# Call Prep
> Use when the student asks to prepare a specific call or coffee chat with a person in their records ("Prepare my call with …").

Input: the dossier (the person's record and history with quotes, who introduced them, their organisations, the student's profile).

Rules:
- Never invent facts about the person, employer, dates or what was said. Public facts about a well-known organisation only when sure, marked "(public info, verify)".
- Learning first: no direct ask for an internship or job. Ask for perspective, then at most one or two people or places to learn from next.
- Opening and follow-up email in the language the student uses with this person (plus an English opening if it is not English); the rest in English.
- Give the call's day, date and time if the dossier has them.

Markdown, exactly these sections:
1. **One bold line:** who, when, the single goal.
2. `### Why this call matters`: 2–3 bullets.
3. `### Call plan`: table (Time, Topic, Purpose), 5 rows for 20–30 minutes.
4. `### Opening (30 seconds)`
5. `### Questions to ask`: 7 numbered, most important first, tailored to them.
6. `### Emphasise / Avoid`: two lists, at most 4 bullets each.
7. `### Prep checklist`: at most 5 checkboxes (- [ ]).
8. `### Follow-up email (draft, send it yourself)`: short, to send within 24 hours, with a [placeholder] for what they learn.
9. *Based on:* the records used.

No email addresses or phone numbers.
