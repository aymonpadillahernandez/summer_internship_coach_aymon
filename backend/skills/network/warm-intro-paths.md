# Warm Intro Paths
> Use when the student asks who in their network could open the door at one target opportunity, and what to ask each person.

Use only the records given ([OPP-n], [CON-n]). Never invent a person, title or relationship.

1. **Candidates:** a path is one person in the records who:
   - works at the organisation;
   - knows someone there;
   - offered an introduction there;
   - or works close enough to the field to point the way.
2. **Rate each** with skill `relationship-strength` (opportunity version).
3. **Rank, up to 3, best first:** strength, then directness (works there > knows someone there > offered an intro > adjacent field).
4. **For each path:**
   - `why_them`: one sentence from the records on how they connect to this opportunity;
   - `ask`: learning first (perspective on the team, an introduction to a named person, process advice). Never a referral from a weak contact;
   - `first_message`: one or two sentences the student could send, in the language they use with this person. The app never sends it.
5. **Two-step paths:** the first step is the student's own contact: ask them for the introduction and offer a short paragraph they can forward.
6. **No real path:** return no paths; in `next_best` say how to find one (ask a strong contact who they know there, or go to an event on the leads).

No email addresses or phone numbers.
