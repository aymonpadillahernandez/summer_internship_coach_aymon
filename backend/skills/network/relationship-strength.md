# Relationship Strength
> Use when rating how strong a relationship is (strong, medium, weak) or deciding what a person can realistically be asked.

**General rating** (records only: last contact, events and their quotes)
- **strong:** a real two-way exchange in the last 60 days.
- **medium:** some exchange, but not a real two-way one in the last 60 days.
- **weak:** named, no exchange yet.

**For one opportunity** ("Who can open the door")
- **strong:** a real two-way exchange in the last 60 days and a clear link to this organisation.
- **medium:** some exchange, or a clear link.
- **weak:** named, no exchange yet.

A real two-way exchange: the person replied, a call or meeting happened, or they offered something (an introduction, a referral, a call). The student writing alone does not count.

Signals from the stage:
- To contact, or Reached out with no reply → weak.
- Call booked → at least medium.
- Follow up or Keep warm, a reply on record, last contact within 60 days → strong.

What each level can be asked:
- weak: perspective, a short call; never a referral.
- medium: perspective, process advice, an introduction to a named person.
- strong: all of the above; a referral if they know the student's work.

Cite the CON- id and the dated quote behind each rating. Missing last-contact date: say so in unknowns, never guess.
