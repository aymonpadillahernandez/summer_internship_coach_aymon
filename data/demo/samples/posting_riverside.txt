Riverside Arena Group — MBA Summer Associate, Business Strategy (Summer 2027)
Location: Boston, MA (hybrid)

About the role
Riverside Arena Group operates three arenas in New England. Our 10-week MBA Summer Associate program places first-year MBA students on the strategy team, working on premium seating, event scheduling and new revenue streams.

Who can apply
Open to full-time MBA students graduating in 2028. Prior consulting, finance or sports business experience is a plus.

How to apply
Submit a resume and a one-page note on one revenue idea for a mid-size arena by @text+12. First-round interviews will be held virtually in December.

Questions? Contact the campus recruiting team through the careers portal.
