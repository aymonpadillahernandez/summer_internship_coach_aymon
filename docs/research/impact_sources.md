# Impact estimate: public sources

Facts behind `scripts/impact_estimate.py` (an ESTIMATE, not a user test). Each was read on its own page on 2026-10-26.
Most are career-office **advice**, not measured averages. No source gives minutes per recruiting task, so those inputs are
author assumptions in the script, each with a low and a high value and a one-line reason. Quotes are under 15 words.

## Gaps

- No career office publishes minutes per task (checking a careers page, finding people at a target, logging a message, preparing a chat, writing a thank-you). Those inputs are author assumptions in the script, each with a low and a high value.
- No primary source gives the average number of coffee chats or applications per MBA student; only advised rates (S3, S6).
- No school calls recruiting a 'full-time job'; the strongest official wording is 'an additional class or part-time job' (S1).
